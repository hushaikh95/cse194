Methods and sources

What the numbers mean

  • Two-bedroom rent is HUD's Fair Market Rent (FMR), an estimate of a modest rental including utilities. It is not the average asking rent.
  • Housing wage is the hourly pay a full-time (40 hr/week) worker needs so that rent is no more than 30% of income.
  • Hours per week at minimum wage = housing wage × 40 ÷ the $16.90 state minimum wage. Some cities set a higher local minimum wage, which this page doesn't show yet.
  • Income needed = annual rent ÷ 0.30. Median renter household income covers the whole household, which may include more than one earner.

Rental areas vs. counties

HUD sets rents by rental area (metro areas and HUD Metro FMR Areas). Counties in the same area share the same figures, for example San Francisco, Marin and San Mateo. For 20 rural counties outside any metro area, this version uses NLIHC's combined non-metro average for California, and those counties are labeled as estimates. Each one will get its own county figure in a later update.

Framing

This page reports figures; it does not argue for any policy. Differences between counties have many possible causes.
